Film · ICICI Foundation

One tiger explains 56 forests

ICICI Foundation protects 56 forests and tiger reserves. The film tells that scale through one female tiger, ST9, at Sariska. I came up with the idea and wrote the script, helped the producer with casting and costume, and made small changes in post-production. It reached 2.6M views and runs across ICICI Foundation's internal and external communications.
